A-104 · Drawing Register

Drawing Register & Revision Control

A Windows desktop application that brings drawing records, revision history, PDF sheets, and distribution logs together. It helps a project team answer which revision is current, who received it, and which transmittal it was issued under.

Drawing issue workflow

Register drawings

Record the drawing number, title, discipline, status, author, scale, and sheet size. Filter the register by discipline or status and link a PDF to the drawing record.

Record revisions

Log each revision with its code, type, and description. Record who issued, checked, and approved it, then review earlier entries in the drawing’s revision history.

Track distribution

Record the revision sent, the recipient and organisation, the transmittal number, and the purpose of issue.

Reporting

Register analytics

Review revision status and progress by discipline in charts, alongside totals for the register and filtered results.

Export records

Export the drawing register, revision log, and distribution list as Excel workbooks, or generate a PDF summary report.

Implementation

Built in C# with WPF and .NET 8. The application stores records locally in JSON, displays charts with OxyPlot, and generates Excel and PDF reports with EPPlus and QuestPDF.
